# Additional Notes

"One of the more vibrant Qatar attractions for tourists, the 40-meter-deep cave exudes phosphorescence and has a unique beauty. The reason for the glow is a geographical phenomenon known as the "desert rose" (clusters of rose-shaped gypsum crystals) formed from gypsum deposits found in the central part of the peninsula. What is more striking is that the fibrous gypsum forms the depth of the Amisfir Caves up to 40 meters, and sometimes emits a faint moon-like phosphorescence."
