# About

Lake Purrumbete, located near Camperdown in Victoria, is a unique, deep volcanic crater lake widely used for scuba diving and freediving training. It offers a maximum depth of 45 metres and is particularly popular for underwater navigation, rock wall explorations, finding lost boat anchors, and deep-water training. Also good for fresh water training for cave diving.

### Key Dive Site Details
Location: Just south of Camperdown, Victoria (roughly a 2-hour drive from Melbourne/Caulfield).Max Depth: 45 metres (147 feet).Bottom Profile: Rock walls, submerged weed beds, and a sandy/muddy basin.Marine Life: Schools of small fish hiding in the weeds, as well as trout and salmon.

### Conditions & Hazards
Visibility: Can be quite poor (averaging 10 metres or less) and it gets dark quickly as you descend, making a reliable dive light essential.Temperature: Water temperatures fluctuate, but cold thermoclines are common at deeper levels, so thick neoprene or drysuits are generally recommended.Access: Easily accessible shore diving from the boat ramp at the Lake Purrumbete Caravan Park or via arranged boat charters.
