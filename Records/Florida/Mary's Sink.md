# Source notes

Mary's Sink is a very small sink just to the East of [Joe's Sink](Joe%27s%20Sink.md).

Both sinks are located on Highway 50 in Hernando County.  Additionally, this sink

is in the area of [Nursery Sink](Nursery%20Sink.md),

Diepoler II and

III

as well as Lost 40.

The average temperature in this system is 68°.  Care should be taken and a

line always run in this system as it is very small and incredibly silty.

The surface pool affords 3 solution tubes to a ~20' disc shaped room.  There is a

piece of rebar in the floor that has line running to the south and into the silt.

I was told that this line runs to a tunnel that actually connects Joe and Mary's.

However, this has not been verified by me.

Be somewhat careful here as it is a local hangout for teenagers using [Joe's Sink](Joe%27s%20Sink.md)

as a swimming hole.
