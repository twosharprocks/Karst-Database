- **Youtube:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3e7cqLf1WUw

(Guy Bryant)
"A short cave dive into a beautiful sink known as Jewel's Sink near Newberry, Florida. This short cave starts out as a large tunnel but quickly turns into a small low passage for a couple hundred feet before ending in breakdown. It's very silty and dusty. Just swimming through it will dislodge silt/dust from the walls and ceiling."
