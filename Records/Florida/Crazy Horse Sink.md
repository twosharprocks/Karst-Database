# Source notes

The sinkhole is in a wooded area and contains trees, limbs, organic debris, horse & cow skeletons.  The sink is surrounded by scrub oaks, palmettos.  Water flows west beneath the river to Lafayette County and into the Lafayette Blue Cave System.  The pool is about 35 feet in diameter with very steep slopes.
