# Notes

- **Vrelo Cave**, Matka Canyon: This is perhaps the most famous cave in the country and where cave diving becomes especially relevant.

- Located just outside of Skopje, Vrelo Cave is a submerged karst cave accessible via the Matka Lake reservoir.
- The cave is still actively being explored by cave divers, with depths beyond **230 meters**, making it one of the **deepest underwater caves in Europe**.
- Only experienced technical divers with trimix capability should consider diving here, as it involves significant depth and decompression obligations.
- Exploration dives have been conducted by international teams, and the full extent of the cave is still unknown.
