# Additional Notes

- **Wikipedia:** https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cova_des_Pirata

(Translated from German) "The entrance to the Cova des Pirata is located 350 meters from the east coast of Mallorca at Cala Falcó within the Cales Verges de Manacor nature reserve . The next town, S'Estany d'en Mas, is 1100 meters to the north, the bay of Cala Varques 750 meters to the south and the historic fortified tower of Son Fortesa 2090 meters to the west.

The cave entrance, with a metal grille door, lies south-east along the path from S'Estany d'en Mas to the buildings of Can Frasquet , under tall trees amidst shrubs in a small sinkhole . [2] From there the main rooms of the Cova des Pirata reach about 140 meters to the south. About 50 meters west of the entrance there is a connection to the Cova des Pont . The greatest width of the cave is 70 meters. [3] The cave entrance is 22 meters above sea level , the height difference of the cave is 33 meters in total."
