# Additional Notes

"Cova de sa Piqueta is a cave in Balearic Islands. Cova de sa Piqueta is situated nearby to the caves Cova des Pont and [Clot de sa Clova Redona](Clot%20de%20sa%20Clova%20Redona.md)."
- **BBC:** https://www.bbc.com/news/magazine-40558067

- **DeeperBlue:** https://www.deeperblue.com/cave-diver-rescued-spending-two-days-trapped-cave/

"Cave divers Xisco Gràcia and Guillem Mascaró had to make the most difficult of decisions on April 15, 2017. Trapped in an air pocket deep in a cave, they only had enough gas between them for one of them to make it out, and the other would have to stay behind, according to the BBC."
