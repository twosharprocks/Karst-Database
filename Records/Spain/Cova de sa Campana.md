# Additional Notes

- **mondoaventura.com:** https://mondaventura.com/es/actividades-aventura-mallorca-pollensa/tours-excursiones-guiadas/espeleologia-cuevas/cueva-terrestre-cova-sa-campana/

"The Sa Campana cave , of great biological and speleological interest, is the second deepest cave (up to -350 meters!) and one of the most beautiful in Mallorca. We can divide it into several sectors of different difficulty, made up of huge rooms and a multitude of formations.

Our route will take us through impressive rooms, full of formations of all kinds: lava flows, cat flaps, gours, wells... all of them spectacular due to their sizes and shapes.

We will certainly be impressed by the size of the cave and the dimensions of its rooms and formations, since there are no other known caves on the island with chambers of such magnitude."
