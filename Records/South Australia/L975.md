# About

2021-Aug: MW visited on foot & photographed. Small fenced area with two yellow Forestry signs marked "L975". Appears L322 refers to larger doline which includes two separate solution tubes, one of which is L975 and another larger one covered by bushes and located north of L975 at these coordinates (provided by Forestry and marked L322).

L975 is very small/shallow - not worth further investigation, but if a larger cave is present underneath it may eventually connect.
