# About

2021-Aug: MW visited on foot and photographed. Sliding steel hatch (No lock?) obscured under canopy of pine tree and covered in pine needles.

2023-Aug: MW and BW unlocked, checked and photographed. Lock is awkward, needed hammer to fully release. Tight solution tube probably 7-8m deep. Possible space at bottom opening into cave, return with wire ladder to check bottom

# Additional Notes

1m diameter hole drops 5.7m to small entrance at bottom. 
- **Forestry SA:** L0309, Thin Roof: N, Fence: Y. Dimensions: Ent. 1m diam x 5.5m deep solution tube. Features: Already fenced
