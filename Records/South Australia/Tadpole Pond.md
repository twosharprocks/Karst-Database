# Source notes

## Lower South East Cave Reference — This spring-fed flooded doline lies adjacent to L247, and is little more than a tadpole-shaped pond about 8 metres in diameter and around 4m deep. Apart from collecting water from the nearby [Hammerhead Pond](Hammerhead%20Pond.md) system, the pond is filled with water from a small fissure-spring in the bowl. The feature contains virtually nothing of interest to cave divers.
