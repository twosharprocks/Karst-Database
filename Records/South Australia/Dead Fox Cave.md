# Source notes

## Lower South East Cave Reference — Page 542

Hidden in a peaceful area of old pine forest, Dead Fox Cave got its name when the author and fellow cave diver Maurice Parry (who pointed out the feature) discovered the remains of said beast during their exploration of September 1987.
The entrance takes the form of a low slide-down under a 2 metre high limestone ledge at the
southern edge of an indistinct degraded doline, and it quickly opens up into a small muddy cham-
ber about a metre high, a few metres wide and 12 metres long.
The water-table barely intersects the cave, flooding the flat floor during the September visit and completely drying out during a subsequent visit some 4 months later, at the height of the local summer season.
