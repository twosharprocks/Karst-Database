# About

2021-Aug: MW visited on foot. Old fencing wire still present, sinkhole filled with considerable tree waste. Small cave/burrow found on Western side, Eastern side inaccessible due to prickly blackberry bushes. Worth further investigation to find potential cave on Eastern side, but will require extensive brush cutting.
