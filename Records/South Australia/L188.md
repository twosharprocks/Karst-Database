# About

L188 with L189 sister feature supposedly nearby under debris?

# Additional Notes

CAVE/KARST FEATURE NUMBER: 5L188
([Unnamed Feature](Unnamed%20Feature.md)).
CEGSA Records provided very little information about 5L188 and its nearby "sister" feature, 5L189. However, when the surrounding forest was felled in late 1992, the author and fellow caver Kevin Mott were able to have a quick look at both features briefly in November of that year.
5L188 proved to be a low, undercut, horizontal crawly-passage about 1-1.5m high, some 6 metres deep and about 8 metres long, running under the edge of a rock face on the western side of an 8 metre diameter surrounding doline. It wasn't possible to locate 5L189 accurately amongst the tree debris, but both features appear to be about the same, judging by word-of-mouth descriptions heard to date.
- **Forestry SA:** L0188, Thin Roof: N, Fence: N. Dimensions: 1-1.5m crawly passage located in rock fa. Features: Alcove 8x6m
