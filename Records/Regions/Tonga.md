# About

Best time is April to October (Dry season). Excellent visibility and the chance to encounter humpback whales during their migration (June to November).

Tonga's geological landscape includes islands with uplifted coral limestone formations, leading to the development of karst topography characterized by caves, sinkholes, and underground drainage systems. Notably, **ʻEua Island** exhibits extensive limestone formations with karst features, including numerous caves formed through the dissolution of carbonate rocks.

# Cave Notes

**[Cathedral Cave](../New%20South%20Wales/Cathedral%20Cave.md) (ʻEua Island)**
- **Depth**: Entrance at approximately 28 meters.
- **Features**: A vast underwater cavern with natural light filtering through openings, creating a cathedral-like ambiance.
- **Access**: Dives are typically organized by local operators, such as Jones Travel & Tours. - **Depth**: Entrances at 3 meters and 12 meters.
