# About

Guadalcanal & Choiseul Karst
- **Why:** Volcanic-limestone islands with known karst features and resurgence-fed streams.
- **Status:** Some dry cave records, but very little underwater exploration.
- **Potential:** Remote karst spring diving with oceanic isolation appeal.
- **Best Season:** May to October.
