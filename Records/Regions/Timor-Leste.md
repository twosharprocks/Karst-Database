# About

- **Why:** Tectonically uplifted limestone karst; similar geology to adjacent parts of Indonesia.
- **Status:** Limited cave documentation; no cave diving.
- **Potential:** First exploration of resurgence systems and flooded karst valleys.
- **Best Season:** May to October. Best month = September

# Trips

- Flights daily Darwin to/from Dili
- Previous exploration by Portuguese cavers
* [“Fatuk-kuak hosi Timor-Lorosa’e” project](https://www.academia.edu/37402660/_Fatuk_Kuak_Hosi_Timor_Lorosae_Caves_of_Timor_Leste) (annually since 2016)
    * “Caves of Timor-Leste”
    * Organised by 4 Portugese Caving clubs
    * [Bakua Sink](../Timor-Leste/Bakua%20Sink.md) (Maubisse, Ainaro), resurgence at [Biribui Spring](../Timor-Leste/Biribui%20Spring.md)?
* Lautém Municipality: [Lene Hara Cave](../Laut%C3%A9m%20Municipality/Lene%20Hara%20Cave.md), [Jerimalai](../Laut%C3%A9m%20Municipality/Jerimalai%20Cave.md), [Ile Kére Kére](../Laut%C3%A9m%20Municipality/Ile%20K%C3%A9re%20K%C3%A9re.md)
* Baucau District: [Lilawy Cave](../Baucau%20District/Lilawy%20Cave.md) (near Kairiri), [Wai-lia-bere](../Baucau%20District/Wai-lia-bere.md) and [Wai-lia-mata](../Baucau%20District/Wai-lia-mata.md) (near Darasula), [Lilawehú](../Baucau%20District/Lilaweh%C3%BA.md) (resurgence near Karavela)
* Manatuto District: Laili
* Bobonaro District: Marobo Hot Springs
