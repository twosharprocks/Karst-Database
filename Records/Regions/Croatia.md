# Cave Notes

Izvor Une, 44.7542, 15.7746, 
- Deepest underwater cave in Croatia; dived to 248m  
[Izvor Cetine](../Croatia/Izvor%20Cetine.md), 43.9203, 16.494, 
- Iconic karst spring; narrow shaft dived to 155m.  
Izvor Gacka (Pećina Vrelo), 44.842, 15.3104, 
- Multiple spring heads feeding Gacka River; partially explored. 
Izvor Rumin, 43.8852, 16.6798, 
- Submerged karst spring in Cetina basin; partially explored.  
Izvor Slunjčica, 45.1150, 15.5953, 
- Spring forming Slunjčica River; extensive karst drainage, largely unmapped underwater.
