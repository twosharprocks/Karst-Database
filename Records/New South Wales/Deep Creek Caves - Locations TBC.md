# About

Three "[Deep Creek](../South%20Australia/Deep%20Creek.md)" caves to locate: North, West, and East

# Additional Notes

Yarrangobilly has three ‘[Deep Creek](../South%20Australia/Deep%20Creek.md)’ Caves: North, West, and East (no, there is no South [Deep Creek](../South%20Australia/Deep%20Creek.md)). West [Deep Creek](../South%20Australia/Deep%20Creek.md) is the master cave for the system, but is next to impossible to access.* North [Deep Creek](../South%20Australia/Deep%20Creek.md) is niiiice, but it is East [Deep Creek](../South%20Australia/Deep%20Creek.md) that is undisputably the best ‘[Deep Creek](../South%20Australia/Deep%20Creek.md)’. All three caves are expected to resurge at Hollin Cave down on the Yarrangobilly River, along with the water from the Eagles Nest system.
